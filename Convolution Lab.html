Long multiplication before carrying

Write digits from least significant to most significant: x = Σ xi10i, h = Σ hi10i, and y = xh = Σ yi10i. Before carries, the coefficient at 10i is a discrete convolution.

yi = Σij=0 xjhi−j   in the carry-free case; otherwise the sum is the raw coefficient before carrying. Out-of-range digits are zero.

Carry caution. To see convolution coefficients directly as decimal digits, keep the inputs mostly to 0s, 1s, and 2s and keep each raw sum below 10. If a sum reaches 10, multiplication still starts with convolution, but carrying hides the raw coefficient.
